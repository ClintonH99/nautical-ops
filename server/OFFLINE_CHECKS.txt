Important current behavior:
- Unattempted edits can coalesce. Once an upload was attempted, further edits
  to that record wait for reconciliation, so an idempotency ID cannot change.
- Offline expiry retains pending work. Current online authorization can sync
  it after expiry, while a revoked/blocked account cannot.
- Browser storage failure must surface as a failed save, never fallback to an
  in-memory success. Browser eviction and user clearing site data remain risks.
- The browser owner or XSS can access local data. Local time checks detect
  observed rollback, not deliberate tampering with the whole device/storage.
