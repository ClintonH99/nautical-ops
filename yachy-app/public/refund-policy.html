Refund Policy

Last updated: March 2025

01

Introduction

This Refund Policy ("Policy") applies to subscription payments made for Nautical Ops vessel plans. By subscribing to a plan, you agree to this Policy. Nautical Ops is committed to fair and transparent handling of refund requests.

02

General Policy

Subscription fees are generally non-refundable. Once a payment has been processed and your vessel plan has been activated, you have access to the Service for the billing period you selected. We do not provide refunds for change of mind, partial use of the Service, or cancellation mid-period.

03

Eligible Refunds

Refunds are available only in the following circumstances:

Double charges: If you were charged twice for the same billing period (e.g., duplicate transactions), we will refund the duplicate charge upon verification.

Wrong plan selected: If you can provide proof that you selected the wrong plan and contact us promptly after the charge, we may, at our discretion, issue a refund or adjust your plan. Proof may include screenshots, transaction records, or other documentation that clearly demonstrates the error.

04

How to Request a Refund

To request a refund, contact us at support@nautical-ops.com with the following information:

1

Your account email address and vessel name

2

Transaction date and a clear description of the issue

3

For double charges: include both transaction IDs or receipts

4

For wrong plan selection: include proof as described in Section 3

05

Processing Times

Here's what to expect after submitting a refund request:

14
Business days to review your request
5–10
Business days to process approved refunds

Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method. The timing of the refund appearing in your account may vary depending on your bank or payment provider.

06

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted within the application or communicated via email. Your continued use of paid features after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated Policy.

07

Contact

For refund requests or questions about this Policy, contact us at support@nautical-ops.com.
