Refund Policy

Effective date: 1 June 2026  ·  Last updated: 27 June 2026

All Nautical Ops subscriptions are purchased through the Apple App Store. Because Apple processes all payments on our behalf, all refund requests must be submitted directly to Apple. The Lumen Digital is unable to issue refunds for App Store purchases.

How to Request a Refund

Apple handles all refund requests for Nautical Ops purchases. Follow these steps:

  • Visit Apple's refund portal Go to reportaproblem.apple.com on any browser.
  • Sign in with your Apple ID Use the same Apple ID that was used to purchase the Nautical Ops subscription.
  • Find your Nautical Ops purchase Locate the subscription charge in your purchase history.
  • Submit your request Select "Request a Refund", choose a reason, and submit. Apple typically responds within 48 hours.

Apple's Refund Policy

Refund eligibility is determined entirely by Apple in accordance with their Media Services Terms and Conditions. Apple may grant refunds at their discretion, typically in circumstances including:

  • Accidental purchases
  • Technical issues that prevented use of the app
  • Unauthorised purchases (e.g., made by a minor without permission)

Apple's refund decisions are final. For more information on Apple's policies, visit Apple Support — Request a refund for apps or content.

Free Trial Periods

Nautical Ops may offer a free trial period before charging your subscription. If you cancel during the trial period, you will not be charged. Any unused portion of a free trial is forfeited when you start a paid subscription. Apple manages free trial eligibility — each Apple ID is entitled to one free trial per subscription product.

Cancellation

Cancelling your subscription prevents future charges but does not entitle you to a refund for the current billing period. You will retain access to Nautical Ops until the end of the period you have already paid for.

To cancel, go to: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Nautical Ops → Cancel Subscription on your iPhone or iPad.

Our Commitment

While we cannot process refunds directly, we are committed to ensuring Nautical Ops delivers genuine value to your crew. If you are experiencing a technical issue or are unhappy with the app for any reason, please contact us before requesting a refund — we may be able to resolve your issue directly.

Contact Us
