Common questions

Anything you want to know.

Nautical Ops is a professional crew operations app built specifically for superyacht and commercial vessel crews. It lets captains and crew log voyages, manage tasks, track maintenance, and run yard periods — all from a single iPhone app, with full web access included.
Nautical Ops is built for professional superyacht crews — captains, heads of department, and crew members across all departments. Whether you're running a 40-metre motor yacht or a 90-metre sailing vessel, the app adapts to your team size and structure.
Yes — every vessel gets a 30-day free trial when they subscribe. You can invite your full crew, log trips, assign tasks, and explore all features before committing to a plan. No credit card required to get started.
Nautical Ops is available on iOS (iPhone and iPad) via the App Store, and as a web app accessible from any browser at nautical-ops.com. Android support is coming soon.
Pricing is based on the number of crew on your vessel — from 1–5 crew up to 40+ crew. Plans are available monthly or annually. Visit our pricing page to see all options and current rates.
All data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted on enterprise-grade infrastructure. Your vessel's operational data is private to your crew — we never share or sell it. Read our full privacy policy for details.
Subscriptions are managed through Apple's App Store. Refund requests are handled directly through Apple within 48 hours of purchase for unused subscriptions. Read our full refund policy for details.
