The story behind the app

Built by someone who's been there.

My name is Clinton Handford. I've been in the yachting industry for about 4 years now, and in that time I noticed something missing — there wasn't a single app that handled everything we need day-to-day. No real all-in-one solution built for crew, by crew.

So I decided to build one. Nautical Ops will never be "done" — I'm constantly updating it, keeping it user-friendly and relevant, not just for professional yacht crew, but for family-run boats and anyone who finds it useful for their maritime needs.

I'm not just building this because I'm in the industry. I'm building it because I believe there's a better way to run a vessel — and I want everyone on the water to have access to it.

Nautical Ops is built for professional superyacht crews — captains, heads of department, and crew members across all departments. Whether you're running a 40-metre motor yacht or a 90-metre sailing vessel, the app adapts to your team size and structure.
