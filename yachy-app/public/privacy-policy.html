Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

01

Introduction

Nautical Ops ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the Nautical Ops mobile application and related services ("Service"). This policy applies to users globally, including those in the European Union (EU), United Kingdom (UK), and California.

02

Data We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us:

Account information Authentication data Vessel & operational data Device identifiers Push notification tokens

This includes your name, email address, position/role, department, optional profile photo, credentials for sign-in, trips, tasks, inventory, maintenance logs, fuel logs, waste logs, pre-departure checklists, watch schedules, shopping lists, contractor information, safety equipment, muster stations, and other vessel operational data. We do not collect financial or medical data.

03

How We Collect Data

We collect data directly from you when you register, sign in, or use the Service. We also receive information from third-party authentication providers (Google, Apple) when you choose to sign in with those services. Push notification tokens are collected when you enable notifications.

04

Purpose of Use

We use your data to: provide, operate, and improve the Service; authenticate your identity and manage your account; enable vessel-specific collaboration with crew members; send push notifications you have opted into; respond to your requests and support inquiries; comply with legal obligations; and protect the security and integrity of the Service.

05

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may process your data: Supabase — hosts our database, authentication, and file storage; Expo — powers push notifications and app infrastructure; Google and Apple — when you use Sign-In with Google or Apple, these providers process authentication data according to their respective privacy policies. We receive only the information necessary to create or maintain your account.

06

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only as follows: vessel crew members assigned to the same vessel can view and manage vessel-specific data; service providers (Supabase, Expo) operate under contractual arrangements that require them to protect your data; we may disclose data when required by law or to protect our rights, safety, or property.

07

Data Retention

We retain your account and operational data for as long as your account is active. When you terminate your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as resolving disputes or enforcing agreements.

08

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest, row-level security in our database to isolate vessel data, secure authentication (OAuth, JWT), and access controls. While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is completely secure.

09

Your Rights — EU/UK (GDPR)

If you are in the European Economic Area or UK, you have the right to:

Access your personal data
Rectify inaccurate data
Request erasure (right to be forgotten)
Receive data in a portable format
Object to or restrict certain processing
Withdraw consent at any time
Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise these rights, contact support@nautical-ops.com.

10

Your Rights — California (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to: know what personal information we collect and how it is used; request deletion of your personal information; opt out of the sale of personal information (we do not sell personal information); and non-discrimination for exercising your rights. Contact support@nautical-ops.com to submit a request.

11

Your Rights — Other Regions

If you are located elsewhere, you may have similar rights to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at support@nautical-ops.com to submit a request. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

12

Children

The Service is not intended for individuals under 16 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at support@nautical-ops.com and we will take steps to delete such information.

13

International Transfers

Your data may be processed in the United States, European Union, or other locations where our service providers operate. We ensure appropriate safeguards (e.g., contractual clauses, adequacy decisions) for transfers outside the EEA/UK as required by applicable law.

14

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by posting the updated policy within the application or via email. The "Last updated" date will be revised accordingly. Your continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

15

Contact

For privacy inquiries, data requests, or questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@nautical-ops.com. We will endeavor to respond within 30 days.
