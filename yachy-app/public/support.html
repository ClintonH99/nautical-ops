Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a vessel?

After signing up as a Captain, follow the on-screen prompts to set up your vessel.

How do I invite crew members?

Go to Settings, tap Crew Management, and use the invite code to add crew.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Email support@nautical-ops.com and we will process your cancellation within 24 hours.

I forgot my password, what do I do?

On the login screen tap Forgot Password and follow the instructions.
